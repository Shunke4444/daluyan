Phone simulator

Demo/drill tool: act as any registered resident — see the SMS they receive and text back. Posts to the same /inbound webhook an Android SIM gateway or engageSPARK would hit. Zero SMS spend.

What this proves

  • Two-way loop end-to-end: alert out → keyword reply in → auto-reply (route status) → console triage.
  • Tagalog/Cebuano aliases route correctly (TULONG→HELP, GAMOT→MEDICINE, LIGTAS→SAFE…).
  • Unrecognized text is logged raw and shown as UNRECOGNIZED — never dropped.
  • In production, replies arrive via the Android SIM gateway webhook (pilot) or engageSPARK toll-free number (graduation) — Semaphore is outbound-only.
